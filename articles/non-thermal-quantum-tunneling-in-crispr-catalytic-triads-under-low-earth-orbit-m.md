### Executive Abstract

      
        Recent orbital experiments conducted aboard the BioGravity LEO Module confirm that buoyant convection on Earth introduces subtle thermodynamic instabilities in macromolecular complexes. In weightlessness (< 0.08 μg), molecular transport transitions strictly to diffusion-governed depletion regimes.
