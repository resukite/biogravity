BioGravity Editorial Policy & Standards

Published by the BioGravity Scientific Governance Board • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Editorial Mission & Purpose

BioGravity (biogravity.space) is an independent open-access scientific publication and educational simulation platform. Our core objective is to demystify complex space biotechnology, microgravity cellular biology, and orbital pharmacology for researchers, educators, students, and life science professionals worldwide.

We operate under the principle of radical transparency, scientific reproducibility, and open-access licensing (Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0).

2. Fact-Checking & Primary Source Hierarchy

Every research dispatch and educational article published on BioGravity must adhere to a strict evidentiary hierarchy:

  • Tier 1 Primary Evidence: Peer-reviewed articles indexed in PubMed, ScienceDirect, Nature, and Cell; official telemetry and flight data from NASA GeneLab, ESA, JAXA, and Roscosmos.
  • Tier 2 Preprints: Preprints from bioRxiv, medRxiv, and arXiv with clear editorial labeling that findings await formal peer review.
  • DOI Attribution: Every factual claim, constant, or experimental protocol must include a permanent Digital Object Identifier (DOI) or verifiable institutional hyperlink.

3. AI Transparency & Human Editorial Review Protocol

In full accordance with Google Search, Google AdSense, and COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) guidelines:

🔬 Mandatory Human Review Gate

While AI research models may assist our editors with synthesizing literature and structuring datasets, no article is ever published automatically without mandatory human review and verification. Every drafted dispatch must be reviewed, fact-checked, verified for citations, and approved by a named human editor before going live.

4. Corrections, Errata & Retraction Policy

We are committed to correcting errors promptly and transparently. If a factual error, calculation inaccuracy, or misrepresented preprint finding is identified:

  • Minor Corrections: Typographical or numerical fixes are noted with a timestamped correction note at the bottom of the article.
  • Substantive Errata: If a paper's conclusion is impacted by revised laboratory findings, an editorial notice is prominently displayed at the top of the dispatch.
  • Reader Submissions: Readers and researchers can report suspected errors directly to biogravity.space@gmail.com. Our board responds within 48 business hours.

5. Scientific Independence & Commercial Non-Bias

BioGravity maintains complete editorial autonomy. Commercial advertisers, sponsors, or affiliate relationships exercise zero influence over our scientific coverage, evaluation of clinical research, or critique of aerospace payloads. Any sponsored or collaborative content is explicitly disclosed with clear visual badges.
