Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosure

Last Updated: October 2026 • BioGravity Governance Office

1. Overview & Scope

At BioGravity (biogravity.space), accessible from https://biogravity.space, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by BioGravity and how we use it.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to biogravity.space and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, anonymized information about user behavior, page views, and visitor technology. IP addresses are masked, and individual user tracking across unrelated websites is strictly disabled.

4. GDPR and CCPA Data Protection Rights

We respect your privacy rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, or request erasure. Contact our compliance desk at biogravity.space@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

5. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at biogravity.space@gmail.com or via X at @Biogravityspace.

Educational Platform & Open Science Transparency Notice

BioGravity is an independent educational science platform and interactive simulation hub exploring theoretical space biology, microgravity fluidics, and quantum biotechnology concepts. All research monographs, interactive computational models, and educational dispatches are prepared strictly for educational inquiry, academic modeling, and open-science exploration under Creative Commons attribution.
