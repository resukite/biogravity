# BioGravity (biogravity.space) Master SEO Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning
BioGravity (`https://biogravity.space`) is positioned as the premier open-access platform for space biology, microgravity biophysics, space medicine, and scientific ethics. By combining peer-reviewed academic rigor with accessible web-native interactive simulators and comprehensive search-optimized dispatches, BioGravity captures multi-intent traffic spanning university students, aerospace researchers, and biotechnology industry leaders.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Search Intent Personas
1. **Academic Researchers & Graduate Students:**
   * *Search Intent:* High-precision informational queries ("microgravity effects on pluripotency", "organ-on-a-chip ISS protocols", "45 CFR 46 minimal risk definition").
   * *Needs:* Citations with DOIs, primary data, mathematical equations, plain-English conceptual explainers.
2. **Aerospace & Biotech Founders / Engineers:**
   * *Search Intent:* Practical and tool-oriented queries ("simulate microgravity shear stress", "space medicine countermeasures timeline").
   * *Needs:* Fast interactive tools, downloadable datasets, regulatory compliance guidance.
3. **Science Enthusiasts & General Public:**
   * *Search Intent:* Trending scientific news ("how astronauts exercise in zero gravity", "space week 2026 events").
   * *Needs:* Engaging visual diagrams, easy-to-read FAQs, Google Discover and News accessibility.

## 4. Key Strategic Differentiators
1. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO):**
   * Incorporate structured data tables, verified key findings sections, and `llms.txt` discovery protocols to maximize quotation in AI Overviews and answer engines.
2. **Scholarly Schema Architecture:**
   * Implement complete `ScholarlyArticle`, `Person` (with ORCID `sameAs`), `Organization`, and `QAPage` markup to establish E-E-A-T signals that traditional blogs cannot replicate.
3. **Interactive Simulator Backlink Magnet:**
   * Embed lightweight web simulators (e.g. clinostat rotation speed calculator) directly inside research dispatches to drive dwell time and natural academic backlink accumulation.
4. **Resilient Publishing Architecture:**
   * Utilize static HTML with pre-rendered SEO metadata, rapid build tooling (`build-seo.js`), and automated XML sitemaps to ensure near-instant crawlability and zero JavaScript rendering delays for search engine spiders.
