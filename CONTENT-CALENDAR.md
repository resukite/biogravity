# BioGravity (biogravity.space) Content Strategy & Publishing Roadmap

## 1. Publishing Strategy & Cadence
* **Pillar Guides (1-2 per month):** 2,500+ words, exhaustive academic citations, primary equations, structured diagrams, and downloadable data.
* **Research Dispatches (2-3 per week):** 1,200–1,800 words, deep-dive analysis of freshly published NASA, ESA, or orbital research results.
* **Daily Space Science News (3-5 per week):** 600–900 words, optimized for Google News (`sitemap-news.xml`), covering payload launches, mission milestones, and biotech experiments in LEO.
* **Interactive Tool Showcases (1 per month):** Walkthrough and benchmark studies tied directly to `/tools/` simulators.

## 3. On-Page Quality Checklist per Article
- [ ] Exactly one `H1`, with primary keyword within first 50 characters.
- [ ] Meta title 50–60 characters; meta description 150–160 characters (must not echo title).
- [ ] Hero image includes explicit `width`, `height`, `alt`, and `fetchpriority="high"`.
- [ ] At least 3 verified academic citations with DOIs.
- [ ] Contextual cross-links to 2 internal articles and 1 tool.
- [ ] `ScholarlyArticle` and `BreadcrumbList` schema validated via Rich Results Test.
- [ ] Content summarized in machine-readable `llms.txt`.
