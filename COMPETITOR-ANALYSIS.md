# BioGravity (biogravity.space) Competitor Analysis

## 1. Competitive Landscape Overview
BioGravity bridges the gap between academic primary research (npj Microgravity, Frontiers) and accessible science journalism (Phys.org, Space.com), enhanced with interactive computational tools.

| Competitor | Domain | Primary Strengths | Weaknesses / Opportunities for BioGravity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **npj Microgravity** (Nature) | `nature.com/npjmgrav` | High domain authority (DA 93+), gold standard peer review | Paywalled or inaccessible technical prose; no interactive tools; slow indexing on non-academic search engines. |
| **NASA OSDR** | `osdr.nasa.gov` | Direct government authority, primary raw omics data access | Dense government portal UX; lacks plain-English synthesis; minimal conceptual Q&A content. |
| **Frontiers in Space Technologies** | `frontiersin.org` | Open-access academic volume, strong author networks | Academic jargon; generic template layout; weak rich snippets / modern GEO readiness. |
| **Space.com / Phys.org** | `space.com`, `phys.org` | High news velocity, strong Google News presence | Surface-level reporting, lacking deep mathematical rigor, reproducible code, and structured academic metadata. |
| **ISS National Lab Blog** | `issnationallab.org` | Mission-specific case studies, industry credibility | Limited content cadence; weak topical clustering and technical schema. |

---

## 2. Content & Keyword Gaps Identified
1. **Targeted Academic Intent ("What statement about...", "Explain the mechanism of..."):**
   * Students, researchers, and biotech founders search for direct, authoritative conceptual answers (e.g., CITI program ethics, protein crystal nucleation kinetics in microgravity).
   * Competitors either summarize too briefly (press releases) or produce dense 40-page PDFs that Google AI engines struggle to extract.
2. **Interactive Biosensing & Microgravity Calculators:**
   * High-intent, recurring developer and researcher traffic searching for "microgravity simulation calculator", "shear stress in clinostat calculator".
   * BioGravity can capture tool-intent queries and earn high-authority editorial backlinks.
3. **Open Access Citation & DOI Attribution:**
   * Providing permanent DOI metadata, downloadable BibTeX/RIS, and CC-BY 4.0 licenses gives BioGravity academic backlink signals superior to typical media sites.

---

## 3. Technical & Schema Edge
* Competitors rarely implement structured `ScholarlyArticle` alongside `QAPage` and `Dataset` schema.
* Competitors lack `llms.txt` and agent-ready machine-readable endpoints.
* By maintaining `llms.txt`, full-text Markdown endpoints, and JSON-LD entity linking (ORCID, Wikidata), BioGravity captures maximum share-of-voice across AI engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews).
