🧬 The Quantum Biology Frontier

Liberating Molecular Biology From the 1-G Gravitational Constraint.

For 3.8 billion years, every cellular process on Earth evolved immersed in a constant 9.8 m/s² gravitational vector. Density differentials induce buoyant convection, shearing fragile molecular assemblies and triggering rapid mechanical decoherence.

BioGravity was founded in 2024 to operate continuous quantum biophysics experiments in Low Earth Orbit (LEO). In weightlessness, buoyancy collapses to zero. Diffusion becomes the sole mode of transport, enabling sub-angstrom crystal growth, extended quantum spin superposition in enzymatic active sites, and true 3D scaffold-free cellular self-organization.
