# Antigravity Brand Rule — Content Scope

> **Status:** ACTIVE & MANDATORY — applies to ALL content generation (titles, scripts, descriptions, ideas, summaries).

## ⚠️ Critical Rule

"Antigravity" is **JUST A BRAND NAME**. It is purely metaphorical — representing curiosity, breaking boundaries, and defying conventional thinking in science.

**It is NOT:**
- A gravity-focused channel
- A space physics channel
- A biogravity channel
- A biology-only channel

**"Biogravity" in the workspace folder name is an old label and does NOT define the channel's scope.**

## Scope

Antigravity is a **comprehensive, multi-disciplinary science content producer** covering ALL fields of science, including but not limited to:

- Physics & Astronomy
- Chemistry & Materials Science
- Biology & Genetics
- Earth Sciences & Climate
- Neuroscience & Cognitive Science
- Computer Science & AI
- Mathematics
- Engineering & Technology
- Medicine & Health Sciences
- Environmental Science
- Quantum Computing
- Robotics & Automation
- Science History & Philosophy

## Mandatory Guidelines

1. **NEVER** restrict topics to gravity, space, or biology unless the user explicitly asks.
2. **NEVER** theme content around the word "gravity" or "antigravity" just because of the brand name.
3. Treat "Antigravity" purely as a brand identity — it implies no topical restriction.
4. Ensure content reflects a **broad, well-rounded, and rigorous scientific scope** across ALL domains of STEM.
5. When brainstorming ideas, draw from the **full spectrum** of science.
6. If previous content was incorrectly narrowed to gravity/space/bio themes, flag and correct it.
