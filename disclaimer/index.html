Scientific & Medical Disclaimer

Last Updated: October 2026 • BioGravity Governance Office

1. Academic & Educational Purpose Only

The content, research dispatches, mathematical formulations, orbital datasets, and experimental protocols published on BioGravity (biogravity.space) are provided exclusively for scholarly, academic, and educational research purposes. BioGravity operates as an open-access platform analyzing non-equilibrium physical and biochemical phenomena in microgravity.

2. No Medical or Clinical Advice (YMYL Safe Harbor)

None of the dispatches, articles, or telemetry summaries regarding protein crystallization, cellular radioprotection, CRISPR-Cas9 catalytic efficiency, or microgravity biology constitute medical, diagnostic, or clinical healthcare advice.

Important Clinical Notice: Always seek the guidance of a qualified healthcare provider or medical professional regarding any medical condition or treatment. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of information read on this scientific repository.

3. Scientific Accuracy & Data Integrity

While our editorial team rigorously verifies citations against established digital object identifiers (DOIs) and preprint archives (including arXiv and peer-reviewed journals), space sciences and biophysical research evolve rapidly. BioGravity does not warrant that all mathematical constants or preliminary laboratory data remain free from subsequent revision by original authors.

4. External Hyperlinks & Citations

BioGravity contains links to external websites, institutional repositories, academic publishers, and digital libraries. We exercise no editorial control over the content, privacy practices, or accuracy of third-party domains. All outbound academic links open in secure new tabs with rel="noopener noreferrer" protection.

5. Advertising & Monetization Transparency

BioGravity participates in the Google AdSense advertising program. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website and other websites across the Internet. For complete details on how advertising cookies are governed and how you may opt out of personalized targeting, please visit our Privacy Policy.
